Who Is NOAH

NOAH is Cognify's oral examiner — an AI that asks, listens, and grades out loud.

Upload a test paper as a PDF or a photo. NOAH reads it, extracts every question, and asks each one aloud to the student — one at a time, by voice. It listens to the spoken answer in real time, evaluates it for conceptual completeness, and moves to the next question.

Every session ends with a full breakdown: a final score, correct/partial/wrong accuracy, struggling topics flagged by subject, and a note on pronunciation and audio clarity — visible to both the student and the institute's admin dashboard.

No multiple choice. No typing. Just a conversation NOAH is having with every student, one question at a time.
